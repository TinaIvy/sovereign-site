What happens in your consultation

  • We talk through your current situation with your dog and your training goals.
  • We give you a detailed plan of action and a personalized quote.
  • We explain how our approach works and why it works, even where other training has not.
  • You leave with everything you need to decide what is right for you and your dog.

How to prepare

  • Think about the two or three behaviors you most want to change, and when they happen.
  • Write down your goals: what should life with your dog look like in a few months?
  • Ask everyone who helps with your dog to join, such as your partner or family.
  • Find a quiet spot where you can take a Zoom call, and have your dog nearby if you can.
  • Jot down your questions. No question is too small.
