Why traditional training often fails

3 common misconceptions about dog training

“I need a trainer to train my dog for me.”

False. If someone else trains your dog, your dog learns to respect that person, not you. Respect is not transferable. You must be actively involved from day one, minute one.

“My dog is a problem and needs to be fixed.”

False. There is nothing wrong with your dog. You treated your dog like you would treat your child, with the best intentions, but dogs don’t understand “human”. A lapse in communication made things turn sour.

“Obedience training will make my dog behave.”

False. “Sit”, “stay” and “come” will not eliminate core behavior issues. All the obedience your dog has learned goes out the window when your dog gets triggered or distracted.
